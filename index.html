The format that travels

Give a drawing,
not a thing.

The Commission exists to be given. He draws a cap for one person — their word hidden in the line where only they know to look — and it is signed, photographed at day 3 and day 6 while it is being made, and handed over. Artists like Devon Rodriguez built an audience of millions on exactly this shape: the making, the giving, the face when it lands. Farhad's version is slower, quieter, and one of one.
