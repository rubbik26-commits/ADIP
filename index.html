Another Day in Paradise · by Farhad Rabbany

Inspi­ration.

noun — “the process of being mentally stimulated to do or feel something, especially to do something creative.”

That is the original site's own opening line, kept — and finally staged the way it deserves. Explore the world of artistic expression and inspiration through hand-crafted, pen-and-ink artwork and illustrative patterns, drawn by Farhad Rabbany in Beverly Hills 90210.

The drawing

One hand.
One line.
No eraser.

Every product in this store starts as a drawing at Farhad's table — meditative pen-and-ink patterns in the Zentangle spirit, drawn in one sitting. The outcome allows both artistic freedom and a finished piece you can wear or hang. That was true on the old site. Here, you can actually see it.
