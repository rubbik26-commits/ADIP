Nº 05 — Process · The proof-of-hand page

Scroll is
the hand.

Keep scrolling, and the drawing below keeps pace with you — down, and the pen advances; up, and it undraws. It isn't a decoration. It is the entire product, performed by your own hand movement.

1 · A blank tile → 2 · One line, no eraser → 3 · Signed. Scroll back up and it undraws — nothing here is a video file; it is one line, drawn by your scroll.

01

The tile

Every piece begins as a blank square of paper on the studio table. The practice started as a daily meditation — a way to sit still with a single line until the noise stopped.

02

The line

One fine-liner. Nothing sketched underneath, nothing erased. The pattern grows the way the tiles grow — outward, mirrored, patient — and a mistake is not removed; it is absorbed into the pattern.

03

The cloth

Finished tiles are printed and embroidered in Los Angeles onto hoodies, caps and a tee — or, for a commission, drawn straight onto a cap, once, for one person.

Your turn — the draw-along tile

Now you.

Draw inside the tile. Your stroke is mirrored the way his patterns grow, so one shaky minute of drawing turns into something that looks deliberate. His took ten thousand hours. Yours takes a minute. Both count.
