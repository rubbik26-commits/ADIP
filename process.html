Nº 05 — Process · the proof-of-hand page

Scroll is
the hand.

Keep scrolling. The drawing below happens at the speed of your scroll — down, the pen advances; up, it undraws. That is not a decoration. That is the entire product, performed by your own hand movement.

1. A BLANK TILE → 2. ONE LINE, NO ERASER → 3. SIGNED. SCROLL BACK UP AND IT UNDRAWS — NOTHING HERE IS A VIDEO FILE; IT IS ONE LINE, DRAWN BY YOUR SCROLL.

01 — The tile

Every piece starts as a blank square of paper on the studio table. The practice began as a daily meditation — a way to sit still with one line until the noise stopped.

02 — The line

One fine-liner. No underdrawing, no eraser. The pattern grows the way his tiles grow — outward, mirrored, patient. A mistake is not removed; it is absorbed into the pattern.

03 — The cloth

Finished tiles are printed and embroidered in Los Angeles onto hoodies, caps and a tee — or drawn directly onto a cap, once, for one person, when it's a commission.
