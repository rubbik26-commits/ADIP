Policies

Shipping.

Processing

Orders are processed in 1–3 business days. The studio does not ship or deliver on weekends or holidays, and high-volume periods can add delay — the confirmation email says where your order stands.

Rates & times

Rates are calculated and shown at checkout before you pay. In this store: US shipping is a $12 flat rate, free on goods of $200 or more. Standard domestic delivery runs 3–7 business days; express 1–3 business days. International shipping is offered; its rates and times are calculated for your address at checkout.

Confirmation & tracking

Every order gets a shipment confirmation email with tracking, active within 24 hours. Prints are made to order (allow 5–7 days before they ship) and the hand-drawn cap follows its own day-3 / day-6 rhythm.
