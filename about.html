Nº 08 — Farhad · The artist, written once

He sits down.
The pen doesn't come up.

Farhad Rabbany works in Beverly Hills 90210, drawing Zentangle-inspired meditation pieces a single line at a time — hearts, waves, suns, and whole words buried inside patterns thick enough to wander through. Every object in this shop traces back to his table.

How the work works

Practice, tile, cloth, studio.

The practiceIt started as a daily meditation — a deliberate way of sitting still, with gratitude as the subject matter. The tiles piled up. Then they covered walls. Then they became clothing.
The tileEach design begins on a single square tile, finished in one sitting with one pen. Sixteen of those tiles grew into the canvas works; three became hoodies; the hearts became the caps.
The clothPrinting and embroidery happen in Los Angeles — nothing is sent overseas, nothing handed to an anonymous print-on-demand service. The $300 cap skips printing altogether: Farhad draws straight onto the cap itself.
The studio1031 S Wooster Street, Los Angeles 90035. Open Monday to Friday, 10:00–16:00. The address appears on every page of this site — an anonymous seller couldn't print that, which is exactly why it's printed.
