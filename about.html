Nº 08 — Farhad · written once, here, and nowhere copied

The man who
never lifts
the pen.

Farhad Rabbany draws in Beverly Hills 90210. Zentangle-inspired, meditative, one line at a time — hearts, waves, suns and words hidden inside patterns dense enough to get lost in. Everything in the ledger started at his table.

THE WORK, WORN — THE GOLDEN HEART HOODIE IN LA LIGHT. THE STUDIO KEEPS THE ORIGINALS; THE CLOTH CARRIES THE LINE OUT INTO THE CITY.

01The practice
Drawing began as a daily meditation — a way to be still, on purpose, with gratitude as the subject. The tiles accumulated. Then the walls filled. Then the clothes.Daily—
02The tile
Every design starts on one square tile, in one sitting, with one pen. Sixteen of those tiles became the canvas works; three became hoodies; the hearts became the caps.One sitting—
03The cloth
The drawings are printed and embroidered in Los Angeles — never shipped overseas, never handed to a print-on-demand anonymous. The $300 cap skips printing entirely: he draws on the cap itself.Los Angeles—
