About us

The world of
artistic expression.

Explore the world of artistic expression and inspiration through hand-crafted, pen-and-ink artwork and illustrative patterns. Another Day in Paradise is the studio of Farhad Rabbany — an artist in Beverly Hills 90210 who draws meditative, Zentangle-inspired line work and turns it into wearable art and canvas.

How a drawing becomes a hoodie.

Farhad sits down at the table and draws until the line says stop — one sitting, one continuous pattern, no erasing. The finished tiles are answered three ways: printed on cotton in Los Angeles, embroidered on caps, or stretched as canvas prints in five sizes. One cap a month leaves the table entirely unique: drawn by hand for a single person, signed and numbered.

The studio is at 1031 S Wooster Street, Los Angeles 90035, open Monday to Friday, 10:00–16:00. The work is drawn in Beverly Hills 90210. Write to info@anotherdayinparadise.la — a person answers, usually Farhad.
