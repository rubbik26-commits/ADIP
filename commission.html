Nº 02 — The Commission · The one that can't repeat

Your cap, drawn once.

The printed cap on the shop wall is $69. This one is $300, and the whole difference is a person: Farhad draws straight onto the cap itself, by hand, across two weeks — your word tucked into the pattern where only you know it lives — and signs it when it's done. Three people a month get one, because three is how fast a hand can honestly draw. That number is his drawing speed, not a marketing device.

What the $300 covers

Drawn, not printedThe pattern goes straight onto the cap in Farhad's hand. No commission ever shares a line with another.
Your wordOne word or name, up to 20 characters, worked into the line where only you know to look.
Photographs as it growsDay 3: the cap half-drawn. Day 6: nearly finished — your word is in there; can you find it?
Signed + certifiedSigned by hand, with a certificate of authenticity made out in your name.
The ledgerYour piece is entered in the public edition ledger — first name, word, month. Opt out whenever you like.
ShippingIncluded in the $300. At this price, postage is not a surprise fee.

The money, in plain terms

Deposit$100 today. Refundable in full until drawing begins on Day 1; after that the piece is personalised and the deposit is spent.
Balance$200 when the drawing is finished. He signs it only after the balance clears — stated now, not sprung on you then.
TimelineTwo to three weeks, from the day your details land (Day 0) to the day it ships.
ReturnsFinal sale — except damage or defect, which are put right. The exception is the rule, and it is printed here.
If he can't finishA full refund. No store credit, no argument.
