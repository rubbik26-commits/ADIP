Nº 06 — Watch · the engine room, printed in the open

Watch him
draw.

This page is the marketing plan, published. Most brands hide theirs. ADIP prints it, because the plan is just the work, filmed honestly: three formats, repeated until one breaks. The numbers below are from an analysis of 1,774 art videos — they are why the formats look the way they do.

Format 01 — the workhorse

The Flash Finish

The video opens on the finished piece for half a second — then rewinds to a blank tile, draws forward at speed, and ends on the piece being worn. Before-and-after structure measured 8.67× the views of a generic opening; opening on raw process measured 1.06× — a plain timelapse is the weakest opener there is. The drawing stays calm. The edit moves: three angles — overhead, pen macro, his face — cut on the beat, 22–35 seconds, and the last frame loops back to the blank tile.

BUT FIRST, THE FLASH: FRAME 4 RUNS FOR HALF A SECOND BEFORE FRAME 1. THE HOOK IS A PROVOCATIVE LINE OF TEXT IN SECOND ONE — MEASURED AT 21.11× — WITH A TRENDING SOUND UNDER IT (22.80×). E.G. "SHE PAID $300 FOR A CAP HE HASN'T DRAWN YET."

Format 02 — the commission engine

The Gift

A commission is drawn for someone specific. The camera films the drawing, the wrapping, the handover — and stays for the face when it lands. Devon Rodriguez repeated this exact shape until it made him the most-followed visual artist on TikTok: 400,000 followers within two days of the format landing, 32M+ since. ADIP's version is slower and one of one — and every Gift video is also the Commission page's sales film, because it shows the day-3 and day-6 photographs, the signing, and the word hidden in the line.

The product this format sells — $300

Format 03 — the conversation

Your Word

Followers comment a word — a worry, a gratitude, a name. Farhad draws the top comment into a pattern and hides the word in the line. The comments become a hunt: hundreds of people searching a drawing for a word is the algorithm's favorite kind of attention, and every video arrives pre-sold, because the audience wrote it. The same mechanic fills the kit's tile wall and the Journal.
