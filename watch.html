Nº 06 — Watch · The engine room, in the open

Watch him

This page publishes the marketing plan most brands would hide — because the plan is only the work, filmed honestly: three formats, repeated until one breaks. The numbers come from an analysis of 1,774 art videos, and they are why the formats are shaped the way they are.

Format 01 — The workhorse

The Flash Finish

It opens on the finished piece for half a second — then rewinds to a blank tile, draws forward at speed, and lands on the piece being worn. The drawing stays calm; the edit does the moving: three angles — overhead, pen macro, his face — cut on the beat, 22–35 seconds, and a last frame that loops back to the blank tile.

Text hook, second one21.11× the views of a plain opening — e.g. "She paid $300 for a cap he hasn't drawn yet."
Trending sound22.80× — a current sound under the drawing, every time.
Before-and-after shape8.67× — finish first, then the making. The structure this format is built on.
Raw process opener1.06× — the plain timelapse is the weakest opener there is. It never leads.

But first, the flash: Frame 4 runs for half a second before Frame 1. The hook lands in second one, or the video doesn't.

Format 02 — The commission engine

The Gift

A commission is drawn for someone specific, so the camera stays for all of it — the drawing, the wrapping, the handover, and the face when it lands. Devon Rodriguez repeated this exact shape until it made him the most-followed visual artist on TikTok. ADIP's version is slower and one of one — and every Gift film doubles as the Commission page's sales film, because it shows the day-3 and day-6 photographs, the signing, and the word hidden in the line.

Format 03 — The conversation

Your Word

Followers comment a word — a worry, a gratitude, a name. Farhad draws the top comment into a pattern and hides the word inside the line. The comments turn into a hunt: hundreds of people searching one drawing for one word is the algorithm's favourite kind of attention, and every film arrives pre-sold, because the audience wrote it. The same mechanic fills the kit's tile wall and the Journal.
