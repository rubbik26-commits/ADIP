Between the wall and the index

One thing here isn't a product.

The Commission is a booking that ends in a cap. He draws yours directly on the cap over about two weeks, and you watch it happen — photographs at day 3 and day 6 as the line grows. $100 holds the slot; $200 falls due before he signs it. Three slots open each month, and when they're gone, they're gone.
