Nº 04 — The Gallery · 16 works · 4 series

Hung by series.

Sixteen works, and every one of them is a drawing first and a product second. They were made in four series, so they hang in four rooms — walk them in order, the way the line learned to walk. Each work comes on canvas in five sizes, $200–$350, and each began exactly the way the Process page shows.

A correction, kept in the open

On the old site, two works were hung wearing another work's photograph: Signature Art and Flow of Serenity were shown with one shared image, and Whirling into Love and Dancing with Gratitude with another. That does not happen here. Every tile in this gallery has been checked against its own file — and Dancing with Gratitude, which still has no photograph of its own, is named in Series II without a tile rather than pictured with the wrong one. A gap on the wall is more honest than a borrowed picture.
