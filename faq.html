FAQ

Questions,
answered.

The original FAQ only talked about hoodies. It still does — those eight answers are here, kept — and the rest of the store gets its answers too.

What makes ADIP hoodies different?

Every design begins as an original hand drawing by Farhad Rabbany — meditative pen-and-ink line work drawn in Beverly Hills 90210, then printed on substantial, high-quality cotton in Los Angeles. No stock art, no templates: the drawing comes first, the hoodie answers it.

Are ADIP hoodies spiritual wearable art?

Yes — that is the studio's own description of the work. The patterns are drawn as a meditation, and the hoodies carry that practice into everyday clothing.

Are the hoodies unisex?

Yes. The hoodies are unisex with a regular, classic fit.

What sizes do the hoodies come in?

S, M, L and XL on the Golden Heart and Paradise Heart hoodies; the Rhythm Heart hoodie is made in S, M and L. The product pages list exactly what exists — nothing is offered that the studio does not make.

Where are the designs created?

By hand, in Beverly Hills 90210, at Farhad's table. Printing and embroidery happen in Los Angeles.

How do I care for my hoodie?

Wash cold, inside out, on a gentle cycle. No bleach. Hang dry or tumble dry low. Do not iron the print or embroidery.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — the studio ships internationally, and US shipping is a $12 flat rate, free on goods of $200 or more. Rates for your order are shown at checkout before you pay.

What is the returns and exchanges policy?

One rule, storewide: 14 days from receipt, unworn and unwashed, in original condition. Email info@anotherdayinparadise.la and the studio answers with the next step.

What sizes do the canvas prints come in?

Five: 11×14 for $200, 16×20 for $220, 18×24 for $250, 24×36 for $300 and 30×40 for $350. Every print is made to order in Los Angeles and ships unframed, with framing guidance in the tube.

How does the hand-drawn cap work?

The Artist-Designed Cap is drawn by hand for one person: $300 total, with a $100 deposit reserving the sitting. You choose a word and a motif on the product page; photos arrive on day 3 and day 6; the $200 balance is due when you approve the day-6 photo, before Farhad signs and numbers it. The drawing is never repeated.

How fast do orders ship?

Processing is 1–3 business days. Standard US delivery runs 3–7 business days; express 1–3. Prints are made to order, so allow 5–7 days before they ship. A confirmation email with tracking follows every order.

How do I reach a person?

info@anotherdayinparadise.la, or the contact page. A person answers — usually Farhad himself. The studio is at 1031 S Wooster Street, Los Angeles, open Monday to Friday, 10:00–16:00.
