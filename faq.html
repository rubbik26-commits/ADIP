Nº 09 — FAQ + Shipping & Returns

The old site contradicted itself — the FAQ said 14 days, the returns page said 30. This page ends that: one returns rule, stated identically on every page of this site, and questions answered in the order a buyer actually asks them.

The rule: 14 days, unworn

The work

Is it really hand-drawn?

Yes. Every pattern begins as a pen drawing by Farhad Rabbany, in one sitting, at the studio in Los Angeles. Apparel reproduces that drawing by print and embroidery; the $300 Commission cap is drawn on directly and is one of one.

Will my piece be identical to the photograph?

Printed and embroidered pieces reproduce the drawing faithfully. The hand-drawn cap never matches any photograph — it is drawn for you, once, and no second one exists.

Who is Farhad?

The artist — Farhad Rabbany, Beverly Hills 90210 / Los Angeles. His page is Nº 08.

Editions & the list

What does "Edition 03" mean?

Pieces are released in numbered editions. When an edition sells through, its pattern retires and is not redrawn. The Paradise List gets every edition 24 hours before the public.

How do I get first access?

Join the Paradise List on the front page. Maximum three emails a month: the edition, one Journal note that sells nothing, and commission slots when they open.

The Commission

How do slots work?

Three a month — that is Farhad's drawing capacity, not a sales tactic. $100 deposit reserves yours; $200 is due when the drawing is complete, before he signs it. Full anatomy on Nº 02.

What word can be woven in?

Any word or short phrase up to 20 characters. It is hidden in the pattern where only you know to look — the day-6 photograph invites you to find it.

Can I return a commission?

Commissions are final sale, because they are personalised — except damage or defect, which are always made right. If Farhad cannot complete yours, you are refunded in full.

Sizing & care

How do the hoodies fit?

Classic fit, true to size, unisex. The Golden Heart and Paradise Heart run S–XL; the Rhythm Heart runs S–L only — printed as such in the ledger, because it is true.

How do I care for a piece?

Wash cold, inside out, gentle cycle. No bleach. Hang dry or tumble low. Do not iron the print or embroidery.

Shipping

When does my order ship?

Processing is 1–3 business days, stated separately from transit so the two are never blurred. US Standard arrives in 3–7 business days; US Express in 1–3. Commissions take 2–3 weeks before shipping — they are being drawn.

What does shipping cost?

The real rate for your address, shown at checkout before you pay — and free US shipping over $200. Commission shipping is included in the $300.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Rates and any duties are shown at checkout before payment, in the open — the #1 reason carts are abandoned is costs discovered late, so nothing here is discovered late.

Returns

What is the returns rule?

14 days from delivery, unworn and unwashed, in original packaging — full refund to your original payment method. One rule: it reads the same here, on every product page, and in the cart.

How do I start one?

Email info@anotherdayinparadise.la or use the contact form — no account required. Return shipping is the buyer's; damaged or defective items are on the studio, always.

Shipping & returns — the whole policy, short

Shipping. Orders are processed in 1–3 business days at the studio in Los Angeles and shipped tracked; the confirmation email's tracking activates within 24 hours. US Standard transit is 3–7 business days, Express 1–3. Shipping is free in the US over $200 and otherwise charged at the real rate shown before you pay. Commissions are the exception in timing only: 2–3 weeks of drawing before they ship, with shipping included.

Returns. 14 days from delivery, unworn and unwashed, original packaging, refunded in full to your original payment method once the piece is back and inspected. Exchanges are handled as a return and a new order, so the piece you want is never held hostage to the piece you sent back. Commissions are final sale except damage or defect. That is the entire policy — there is no second version of it anywhere on this site.
