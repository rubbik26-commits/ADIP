Nº 09 — FAQ + Shipping & Returns

Every answer,
in the open.

The old site argued with itself — one page allowed 14 days for a return, another allowed a different number entirely. That ends here: one returns rule, worded the same on every page of this site, and the questions answered in the order buyers actually ask them.

The rule: 14 days, unworn

The work

Is it really hand-drawn?

Yes — genuinely. Each pattern starts as a pen drawing Farhad Rabbany makes in a single sitting at the Los Angeles studio. Clothing carries that drawing by print and embroidery; the $300 Commission cap is drawn on directly, and only one exists.

Will my piece be identical to the photograph?

Printed and embroidered pieces follow the drawing faithfully. The hand-drawn cap is the exception by design: it never matches a photograph, because yours is drawn once, for you, and there is no second one.

Who is Farhad?

The artist — Farhad Rabbany, of Beverly Hills 90210 and Los Angeles. Meet him →

Editions & the list

What does "Edition 03" mean?

Releases come out in numbered editions. Sell through an edition and its pattern is retired for good — never redrawn. Paradise List members see every edition 24 hours ahead of everyone else.

How do I get first access?

Join the Paradise List on the front page. You'll get at most three emails a month: the new edition, one Journal note with nothing for sale in it, and word when commission slots open.

The Commission

How do slots work?

Three per month — that's the limit of what Farhad can draw, not a marketing device. A $100 deposit holds your slot; the remaining $200 falls due once the drawing is finished, before he signs it. The full anatomy →

What word can be woven in?

Any word, or a short phrase of up to 20 characters. It gets hidden inside the pattern where only you know to look — the day-6 photograph is your invitation to hunt for it.

Can I return a commission?

Commissions are final sale — they're personalised — with one standing exception: damage or defect is always put right. And if Farhad can't complete yours, the refund is full.

Sizing & care

How do the hoodies fit?

Classic fit, true to size, unisex. Golden Heart and Paradise Heart run S–XL; Rhythm Heart runs S–L only — and the shop says so plainly, because it's true.

How do I care for a piece?

Wash cold, inside out, on a gentle cycle. No bleach. Hang it dry, or tumble on low. Keep the iron off the print and the embroidery.

Shipping

When does my order ship?

Processing takes 1–3 business days, and it's quoted separately from transit so the two never blur together. US Standard lands in 3–7 business days; US Express in 1–3. Commissions ship after 2–3 weeks — they're being drawn in the meantime.

What does shipping cost?

The true rate for your address, shown at checkout before you pay — and US shipping is free over $200. On commissions, shipping is already inside the $300.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Rates and any duties appear at checkout before payment, out in the open — carts die when costs show up late, so here, nothing shows up late.

Returns

What is the returns rule?

You have 14 days from delivery — unworn, unwashed, in the original packaging — for a full refund to your original payment method. One rule, and it reads identically here, on every product page, and in the cart.

How do I start one?

Email info@anotherdayinparadise.la or use the contact form — no account needed. Return shipping is the buyer's; anything damaged or defective is the studio's cost, always.

Shipping & returns — the whole policy, short

Two paragraphs.
That's the policy.

Shipping. Orders are processed at the Los Angeles studio within 1–3 business days and sent tracked; the tracking in your confirmation email goes live within 24 hours. US Standard transit runs 3–7 business days, Express 1–3. US shipping is free over $200; otherwise you pay the real rate, shown before you pay. Commissions differ on timing only: allow 2–3 weeks of drawing before they ship, with shipping included.

Returns. Within 14 days of delivery — unworn, unwashed, original packaging — you get a full refund to your original payment method once the piece is back and inspected. An exchange runs as a return plus a fresh order, so the piece you want is never held hostage to the one you sent back. Commissions are final sale apart from damage or defect. That's the entire policy; no second version of it exists anywhere on this site.
