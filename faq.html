FAQ + policies

Every answer, in the open.

One returns rule, prices on everything, totals before checkout. The rest:

What is Another Day in Paradise?

Hand-drawn meditation art by Farhad Rabbany of Beverly Hills 90210, made wearable and hangable: hoodies, caps, canvas prints, a drawing kit, and one-of-one hand-drawn cap commissions. It is art, not food — a London chef uses a similar name.

How much is shipping?

US shipping is a flat $12, and free on goods of $200 or more. The bag shows your exact total before you check out — no surprises at the last step.

What is the returns rule?

One rule, storewide: 14 days. Unworn, unhung, unopened kits. Email info@anotherdayinparadise.la and the studio answers with the label.

How does the commission work?

$300 total. A $100 deposit reserves one of three monthly sittings. You choose cap color, motif, and a word woven into the drawing. Photos arrive on day 3 and day 6; the $200 balance is due when you approve the day-6 photo, before he signs and numbers it. 2–3 weeks door to door. The drawing is never repeated.

Are the prints framed?

Prints are archival canvas, made to order in Los Angeles, and ship unframed; framing guidance comes in the tube. Five sizes: 11×14 $200, 16×20 $220, 18×24 $250, 24×36 $300, 30×40 $350.

How do the hoodies fit?

True to size, in a substantial cotton. The Golden Heart and Paradise Heart list S-XL; the Rhythm Heart lists S-L. When in doubt, the studio answers sizing questions at info@anotherdayinparadise.la before you order.

What is the $39 kit?

Twelve cards in Farhad's hand — six finished drawings to study, six blank tiles to answer — a fine-liner, and the studio's one-page guide. Zentangle-inspired (Zentangle is a trademark of its creators); the method on the cards is Farhad's.

Where is everything made?

Drawn in Beverly Hills 90210. Printed and embroidered in Los Angeles. The studio is at 1031 S Wooster Street, Los Angeles, open Monday to Friday, 10:00–16:00.

How do I reach a person?

info@anotherdayinparadise.la. A person answers — usually Farhad himself.
