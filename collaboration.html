Limited Edition Collaboration

Skyline × ADIP.

Beverly Hills meets Manhattan. Seven of Farhad Rabbany's pieces, recreated as a limited edition with Skyline Properties — his artistic designs exactly as he made them, with the New York skyline added the only way it belongs on his work: drawn in his own line style, inside a circle, SKYLINE PROPERTIES beneath it.

How it works

His art stays.
The skyline joins it.

Nothing of Farhad's is replaced, covered or redrawn. The collaboration adds one drawing to each piece: the Manhattan skyline in the same fine pen lines he uses for his hearts and waves, set in a circle with the Skyline Properties name under it — on the brim of the caps, beneath the artwork on the hoodies and the tee.

The edition

Seven pieces.
One run.

The three hoodies ($175), the Signature and Paradise caps ($69), the Zentangle Spirit tee ($75), and the hand-drawn Artist-Designed Cap ($300) — the same pieces, the same prices as the studio's own line, issued once as the Skyline × ADIP limited edition. Same store rules as everything here: US shipping $12 flat, free at $200 of goods; one returns rule, 14 days.
